**B/R formula summary (from bylaws + code):**
- Opening bid: SD minimum rounded up to $100k (ceil_100k)
- Salary: high_bid × (1 - 0.05 × (years - 1)), floored at SD minimum, rounded to $10k
- Eligibility: all contracted players EXCEPT rookie/UDFA not in final year
- GM options: retain_restructure, retain_revert, revert_transfer (prohibited for expired tagged players), buyout_transfer
