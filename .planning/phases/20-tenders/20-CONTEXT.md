## How This Should Work

Same proven pattern as Phases 18 (Franchise Tags) and 19 (Extensions): validate eligibility counts first, then pricing cell-by-cell against the spreadsheet. Two plans — 20-01 for eligibility validation, 20-02 for bid price validation.

Tender rules mirror the NFL CBA. The ADL tries to follow the real CBA structure for restricted and exclusive-rights free agents.
