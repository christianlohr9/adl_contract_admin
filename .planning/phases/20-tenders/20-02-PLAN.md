**Tender pricing formulas (from bylaws + contracts.json):**

ERFA salary:
- MAX(Veteran Minimum, 1.10 × previous salary)
- 1-year contract, exclusive rights, no auction

RFA bid prices (all rounded DOWN to nearest $100k):
- FRFA: MAX(NFL FRFA price (8.046M in 2026), 2.20 × previous salary)
- SRFA: MAX(NFL SRFA price (5.767M in 2026), 1.65 × previous salary)
- ORFA: MAX(NFL ORFA price (3.674M in 2026), 1.10 × previous salary)
- RRFA: NFL RRFA price (3.520M in 2026) — always the NFL price, no multiplier floor

**NFL RFA prices for 2026 (from contracts.json):**
- FRFA: 8.046, SRFA: 5.767, ORFA: 3.674, RRFA: 3.520

**Rounding:** All RFA opening bids rounded DOWN to nearest $100k (FLOOR_100K)
