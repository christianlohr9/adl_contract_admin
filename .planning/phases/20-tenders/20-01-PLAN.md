**ERFA eligibility rules (from bylaws X-D):**
- 0 remaining contract years
- Previous contract signed at ≤ ADL veteran minimum in one of previous two league years ($0.9m in 2024, $1.0m in 2025)
- Previous contract must NOT be an ERFA contract
- Player does NOT enter RFA auctions

**RFA eligibility rules (from bylaws X-C):**
- 0 remaining contract years
- Expiring contract must NOT be 4+ years old
- Must NOT be a previous RFA contract
- Must NOT include premium contract types (EFT, NEFT, TT, NEFToff, TToff, FRFA, SRFA, ORFA, RRFA, B/R, EXT, 5YO) from 2021 or earlier
- Must NOT be an expired multi-year UFA from 2023 or earlier

**Shared allotment:** ERFA + RFA share 2 tenders per team per season (but allotment is not relevant for eligibility validation — only for actual usage)
