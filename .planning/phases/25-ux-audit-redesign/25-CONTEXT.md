# Phase 25: UX Audit & Redesign - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-04-04
**Status:** Ready for research

<vision>
## How This Should Work

When a GM opens the app, they're greeted with a dark, modern splash screen showing all 32 team logos on a dark background. They click their team and they're *in* — immediately it feels like THEIR app. Team accent colors appear in highlights, badges, and the header, personalizing the experience without overwhelming the consistent dark base theme.

The home view is action-oriented: prominent countdown to the next deadline ("Extension deadline in 3 days"), pending actions, and what needs attention NOW. Rosters are accessible but the spotlight is on deadlines and actionable items. It should feel like a real fantasy sports product — think Sleeper's dark UI with bold typography and clean data presentation.

The sidebar navigation stays but gets elevated — clean icons, better grouping, team branding at the top. When GMs use contract tools, results show the final number prominently with a brief summary of key inputs, and detailed calculation breakdowns available on expand. For smaller result sets (tenders, tags), card-based layouts work great. For larger sets (extensions), a polished table keeps things scannable.

The overall vibe: commissioners see this and think "this is legit." No one should look at it and think "someone's side project."

</vision>

<essential>
## What Must Be Nailed

- **Visual credibility** — This is THE thing. If it looks like a real product, commissioners will trust it over the spreadsheet. If it looks homemade, they won't. Every pixel matters.
- **Team-branded experience** — Accent colors (highlights, badges, header) make each GM feel like the app is theirs. Dark base theme stays consistent.
- **Sleeper-inspired modern sports aesthetic** — Dark mode, bold typography, clean data presentation. It should feel like a fantasy sports product, not an admin tool.

</essential>

<boundaries>
## What's Out of Scope

- No new features — pure visual/UX overhaul of existing functionality
- No mobile optimization — desktop-first, mobile can come later
- No user accounts/auth — keep the current team-selection model
- No new data or calculations — just make what exists look incredible

</boundaries>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- **Splash screen team picker** — All 32 team logos/helmets on dark background as the entry point. Click yours, you're in. Sets the "real product" tone immediately.
- **Sleeper app** as the primary design reference — dark theme, bold accent colors, clean data layouts
- **Accent colors only** for team branding — not full theming. Dark base stays consistent, team colors appear in highlights, badges, header bar
- **Prominent deadline countdown** on dashboard — big, bold, can't-miss-it urgency
- **Adaptive result layouts** — cards for small result sets (ERFA/RFA tenders, tags), polished tables for large sets (extensions)
- **Result + summary** for calculations — final number prominent, key inputs summarized, detailed breakdown on expand
- **Player photos** — nice to have, use if easy but don't block the redesign for them

</specifics>

<notes>
## Additional Context

The primary audience for "buy-in" is the commissioners. They need to look at this and feel confident it replaces the spreadsheet. Visual credibility is the wedge — if it looks professional, the accuracy (already proven at 99.7% match rate) will seal the deal.

The app already has all the functionality needed. This phase is purely about making it look and feel like a product worth trusting with league operations.

</notes>
