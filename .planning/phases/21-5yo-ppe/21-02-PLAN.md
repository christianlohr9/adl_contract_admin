<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Validate PPE escalator levels and ineligibility flags</name>
  <files>src/app/services/buyouts.py, src/app/services/eligibility.py</files>
  <action>
For each of the 160 rounds 2-5 picks in PPE5YO tab (rows 19-98, both conferences):

1. **Extract spreadsheet data:** Player name, position, TSP RK, TagLvl (SRFA/ORFA/N/A/#N/A), INELIG (Cont/Pos/empty), PRICE

2. **Look up player in app DB** by name matching

3. **Call calculate_ppe()** for each player and compare:
   - App's escalator_level → spreadsheet TagLvl mapping:
     - level_3 → SRFA
     - level_1_2 → ORFA
     - eligible but escalator_salary is None (current > escalator) → N/A
     - ineligible → #N/A or INELIG flag
   - App's ineligibility_reason → spreadsheet INELIG:
     - Position PK/PN → "Pos"
     - Not in year 4 / no active contract → "Cont"

4. **Report results** as markdown table:
   - Player | Pos | Pick | SS TagLvl | App Level | SS INELIG | App Inelig | Match?

5. **Fix discrepancies:** If level or ineligibility mismatches found, investigate root cause. Common issues:
   - Percentile threshold (≥0.75 for Level 3)
   - Player not having scores → percentile defaults to 0.0
   - Contract year calculation (signed_season vs season)
   - Players already actioned in DB (post-action vs pre-action spreadsheet snapshot)

Key considerations:
- PPE uses PRIOR season (2025) performance data, same as 5YO
- N/A in spreadsheet could mean: eligible but rank too low for any escalation, OR eligible but current salary already exceeds escalator — investigate which
- #N/A likely means formula error due to missing data (player not on roster, no scores)
- "Cont" ineligibility = player has a contract that makes them ineligible (not in year 4, or already extended/re-signed)
- Already-actioned players in DB may show different eligibility than spreadsheet snapshot (same pattern as phases 18-20)

2. **Report results** as markdown table:
   - Player | Pos | SS TagLvl | SS Price | App Price | Match?

3. **Fix discrepancies:** If prices don't match:
   - Check if spreadsheet uses raw NFL RFA price vs MAX(NFL, multiplier × salary)
   - Check rounding (round_to_10k vs raw)
   - Check which season's NFL RFA prices are used (2026)
   - Verify multipliers: SRFA=1.65, ORFA=1.10

Key observations from spreadsheet data:
- Many SRFA prices show exactly 5.766573577 (= NFL SRFA 2026 price)
- Many ORFA prices show exactly 3.674518447 (= NFL ORFA 2026 price)
- This suggests for most PPE players, NFL floor exceeds multiplier × salary (low rookie salaries)
- But some may have higher salaries where multiplier × salary exceeds NFL price — verify these cases
